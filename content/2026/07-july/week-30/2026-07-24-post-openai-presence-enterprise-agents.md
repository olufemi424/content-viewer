## Hook
OpenAI just launched a product that makes enterprise agents feel less like demos and more like controlled work systems.

## Why this matters
This matters because most AI agent talk still focuses on what the model can say, not what the system can safely do. OpenAI Presence shifts the conversation toward production rules: limited access, approved actions, human escalation, and post-launch improvement. For creators, that gives a clearer story than another generic model release. For solo builders and operators, it is a practical blueprint for shipping agents that handle real customer or internal workflows without pretending humans disappear from the loop.

## Mechanism
OpenAI says Presence is available for voice and chat agents and is designed for customer support, outbound sales, and higher-risk internal workflows. The official launch page says each deployment starts with a defined job, gives the agent only the systems and information needed for that task, and wraps model reasoning with policies, guardrails, approvals, and escalation rules. OpenAI also says teams can simulate conversations before launch, monitor live performance, and use a Codex-based improvement loop after deployment. The key mechanism is not "give the agent every tool." It is: define the job, narrow the permissions, approve the actions that matter, and escalate the rest.

## Proof/use case
The proof is that OpenAI positioned this as a live enterprise product rather than a lab preview and published concrete examples like billing support, insurance claims, sales workflows, and employee IT service requests. IT Brief Asia independently reported that Presence is being offered to eligible enterprise customers through a limited general-availability program with Forward Deployed Engineer support rather than as a self-serve toy. For Olufemi's audience, the useful use case is not "replace your whole team." It is using the Presence pattern to understand how real agent systems will likely ship: narrow task scope, policy-backed actions, and clean handoff to humans when confidence or risk changes.
