## Hook
Notion just fixed one of the most annoying parts of building automations in Notion: finding the right IDs and jumping between surfaces.

## Why this matters
For creators, solo builders, and operators, a lot of workflow automation dies in setup friction. You know the use case. Turn a content database into an automation. Connect a worker. Pass a page ID into a script. But the annoying part is usually not the logic. It is finding the right object IDs, opening another portal, checking logs somewhere else, and breaking your flow before you even build anything useful. This update matters because Notion moved more of that setup work into the product itself.

## Mechanism
On August 19, 2026, Notion added a new Developer section inside the workspace sidebar where users can manage Workers, connections, and personal access tokens and view deployed Workers plus logs for each run. In the same release, Notion added a developer bar that can copy the ID or full API object for a page, database, block, workspace, or user in one click. The workflow shift is simple: instead of leaving your workspace to gather metadata and switch into a separate developer surface, you can stay inside Notion, grab the object you need, and keep moving on the build.

## Proof/use case
The proof here is product-level, not speculative. Notion's August 19 release note says the new sidebar section manages Workers, connections, and personal access tokens and shows deployed Workers and logs. Notion's main What's New page repeats the same feature set and the exact path to enable developer features in settings. For Olufemi's audience, the use case is immediate: if you are wiring a content tracker, CRM board, or internal ops database to scripts or agents, one-click object IDs and in-product logs remove a bunch of low-value setup work. This is not a giant headline AI launch, but it is the kind of product change that makes lightweight builder workflows more likely to actually get finished.
