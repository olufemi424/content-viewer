## Hook
Most AI video launches are just prettier demos. FLUX 3 Video is more interesting because it ships native audio, keyframes, continuation, and draft pricing inside one API workflow.

## Why this matters
For creators, solo builders, and operators, the real bottleneck is not generating one flashy clip. It is turning video generation into something repeatable enough to test, budget, and integrate into a content pipeline. Black Forest Labs made FLUX 3 Video generally available on August 4, 2026, and the practical signal is not only quality. It is the workflow shape: one system that can do text-to-video, image-to-video, and video continuation with synchronized audio, plus a cheaper draft mode before a full render. That makes it easier to prototype faster, decide what is worth keeping, and treat AI video more like production infrastructure than a one-shot toy.

## Mechanism
According to Black Forest Labs' product post and documentation, FLUX 3 Video supports text-to-video, image-to-video with pinned keyframes, and video continuation on the same endpoint. The docs say clips can run up to 20 seconds in a single request at HD or Full HD, with synchronized audio generated in the same call. The pricing page adds the other operational detail that matters for this audience: video is billed per second, and draft renders cost about a third of full renders. In plain English, that means the usable innovation is not just "video with sound." It is a programmable loop where you can test a shot cheaply, keep the draft you like, and only then pay to enhance it.

## Proof/use case
The proof is in the first-party materials and the market reaction around them. Black Forest Labs' August 4 post says FLUX 3 Video is now generally available and can create clips up to 20 seconds with native audio at 720p and 1080p. The docs back that up with concrete API examples for text prompts, keyframes, and continuation, while the pricing page spells out the actual cost model for drafts versus full renders. VentureBeat's August 4 coverage adds outside corroboration that the launch is being framed as a production-oriented release, not just another model benchmark story. The practical use case for Olufemi's audience is straightforward: if you already make short-form content or product demos, FLUX 3 Video is worth watching because it reduces the friction between ideation, previewing, and shipping a usable shot.
