## Final record-ready script

Stop rebuilding the same competitor report every Monday.

A creator recently showed a Qoder task that tracks Cursor, Windsurf, and GitHub Copilot across official updates, social posts, Reddit, and Product Hunt, then saves a structured report. Qoder's own documentation now uses a weekly competitor tracker as a scheduled-task example.

The useful part is not the timer. It is the research contract. Give the agent a fixed company list, approved sources, a date window, required links, and sections like feature updates, market moves, and user sentiment. Then ask it to compare this week's report with last week's and highlight only what changed.

But do not automate the conclusion. Run the workflow manually first. Check whether every claim links to evidence, review failed runs in the history, and keep pricing, positioning, or publishing decisions behind human approval.

That turns competitor research from a recurring scavenger hunt into a review queue. The agent gathers and organizes; you decide what matters.

## Risk check (claims needing cautious phrasing)

- Describe the social post as a creator demonstration, not independent proof of accuracy or time savings.
- Qoder documents scheduled tasks, run history, and the weekly competitor-tracker example; it does not guarantee that every source will be accessible or every report will be correct.
- Local scheduled runs require Qoder to be running with the device awake and required access available.
- Treat social sentiment as a noisy input, not a representative market measurement.
- Keep strategic conclusions, external publishing, account changes, and sensitive actions behind human review.
