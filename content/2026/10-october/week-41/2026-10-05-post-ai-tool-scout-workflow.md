## Final record-ready script
Stop collecting AI tools. Make an agent test them.

One creator built a tool scout that finds new launches, checks them against his archive, opens the remaining products, and tests whether their claims hold up. He gets a shortlist, then decides what is worth covering.

That last step matters. Discovery is cheap; verification is the work. A useful scout needs a fixed rubric: Is this genuinely new? Does the core feature work? Is it relevant to your audience? And what evidence did the test produce?

This pattern matches Anthropic's guidance for effective agents: let tools gather ground truth, add clear stopping conditions, and pause for human judgment. In the demonstration, the agent even asked before getting write access to the creator's archive.

Start with read-only access, a three-tool limit, and saved screenshots or notes for every verdict. Let the agent reduce the pile. Keep access changes and publishing decisions human.

Save this and build your own tool-scout rubric.
