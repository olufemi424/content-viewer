## Final record-ready script
This is the finished result: one plain product photo turned into three marketplace-ready images, plus a draft listing block you can review before it goes live.

Start with a clean photo and a small fact sheet: product name, materials, size, colors, price, and the claims you are actually allowed to make. Upload both to your image tool, then ask for three specific views: a clean hero image, a lifestyle scene, and a close detail shot. Keep the product shape, label, color, and text locked. Change the background and camera angle, not the thing you are selling.

For repeat work, put the photo, fact sheet, prompt template, and output folder behind an n8n workflow. It can prepare the image requests and save the drafts, but keep the final image check and listing approval human-owned.

Before anything gets published, put the original beside each generated image in a comparison sheet. Check the label text, color, proportions, shadows, and claims. If the model changes the product or invents a feature, reject the image.

The result is a reusable product-image pack instead of another reshoot. DM WORKFLOW for a free workflow audit.

## Risk check (claims needing cautious phrasing)
- Say "marketplace-ready draft" or "listing image pack," not "guaranteed marketplace compliant" or "guaranteed to convert."
- Image models can alter labels, measurements, textures, colors, and small text; every output needs a visual comparison before publishing.
- Product facts and marketing claims must come from the approved fact sheet. Do not let the model invent materials, certifications, ingredients, prices, or performance claims.
- n8n can orchestrate a workflow, but tool availability, API access, model limits, image costs, and exact node behavior depend on the configured services and plan.
- YouTube view counts and GitHub repository counts are point-in-time attention and implementation signals, not proof of sales or productivity gains.
