## Audience + difficulty
- **Audience:** founders, product teams, agencies, freelancers, and creators who need a product launch film or feature demo.
- **Difficulty:** intermediate. The skill depends on a coding agent, a local rendering setup, and a real product or screenshot reference.
- **Input:** product screenshots or a reference video, a short launch concept, brand assets, and the feature or workflow to demonstrate.
- **Output:** a 10–30 second continuous-motion product film where each beat grows out of the previous one instead of cutting between unrelated slides.
- **Tools:** OneTake, Claude Code or Codex, HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Playwright, Chromium, Python, and FFmpeg.
- **Finishing detail:** run the continuity oracle before showing the film to a human. It checks whether beats carry across boundaries instead of merely replacing one another.

## Final record-ready script
Want to turn app screenshots into a continuous product film? Let me show you how.

Start with a short launch idea, your product screenshots, and the moment you want people to remember. Install the OneTake skill for Claude Code or Codex, then ask it for a 15-second launch film. First, it turns the screenshots into a real HTML version of the interface instead of recording a screen. Second, you describe the beats and what should carry from one beat into the next. A prompt bar can open into the app, or a card can grow into the page. Third, OneTake renders the motion and runs its continuity check. If the shots only replace each other, the film fails. The finishing detail is the handoff: make one object survive each transition. Use this for launch films, feature demos, and product teasers. Follow for more creative AI workflows.

## Risk check (claims needing cautious phrasing)
- Report the GitHub star count as an observed snapshot; it changes over time.
- Describe OneTake as a coding-agent skill and motion-film workflow, not as a one-click video generator.

- OneTake requires a local setup including Python, Playwright/Chromium, NumPy, SciPy, Pillow, Matplotlib, FFmpeg, and Node. Do not present installation as effortless.
