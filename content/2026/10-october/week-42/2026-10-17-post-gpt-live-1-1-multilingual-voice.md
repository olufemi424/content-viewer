## Final record-ready script
Your multilingual voice workflow just got a practical upgrade.

OpenAI is rolling GPT-Live-1.1 into ChatGPT Voice, Codex, and dots. The update adds new voice options for French, German, Hindi, Mexican Spanish, Korean, and Japanese, with OpenAI claiming more natural accents and fewer repetitive status phrases while the system searches.

Take one approved script, translate it, then run the same thirty-second passage through the language variants you actually publish. Have a native speaker review pronunciation, pacing, names, and meaning before you reuse the audio or build it into an agent.

The release notes say voice choices can vary by region and account, and the original announcement said API support was coming soon. So do not design an automated production pipeline around access you have not verified.

Save this as your multilingual voice test: one script, one reviewer, six checks.

## Risk check (claims needing cautious phrasing)
- Attribute accent improvements and reduced repetitive status phrases to OpenAI; they are product claims rather than independent quality findings.
- Describe the release as adding voices for six language variants, not as comprehensive support for every accent or dialect in those languages.
- Voice availability can vary by region and account, according to OpenAI's release notes.
- The October 15 announcement said API availability was coming soon; viewers should verify current API access before designing an automated workflow.
- A native-speaker review remains necessary because a product rollout does not guarantee correct pronunciation, pacing, names, or translated meaning in every case.
