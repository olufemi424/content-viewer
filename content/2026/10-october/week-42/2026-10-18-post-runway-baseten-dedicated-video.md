## Final record-ready script
Your video model can now get its own GPUs.

Runway’s Gen-4.5 generation model and Aleph 2.0 editing model are now available for dedicated deployment through Baseten. For teams producing video at scale, that means an isolated, single-tenant environment instead of sharing the workload plane with other customers.

The setup includes reserved GPU capacity with autoscaling. Teams can also license the model weights from Runway, fine-tune them on their own data, and deploy the customized version through Baseten.

A brand producing many campaign variants could customize for its visual guidelines, reserve capacity for launch week, and keep the deployment isolated. Aleph 2.0 can edit multi-scene video up to thirty seconds at 1080p, while Gen-4.5 handles generation from text or images.

This is enterprise infrastructure, so pricing and results will depend on the deployment. Save this as a checklist: isolation, capacity, customization, then output review.
