## Final record-ready script
Turn your research folder into a clickable webpage.

A creator recently demonstrated a workflow that starts with sources in NotebookLM and ends with an interactive page. The important caveat is that NotebookLM is not publishing the website by itself. It helps organize and ground the material, then Gemini Canvas handles the web prototype.

Here is the useful workflow. Add a narrow set of sources to NotebookLM, generate a structured brief with the sections, facts, and citations you want to preserve, and verify those details against the originals. Then bring that brief into Gemini Canvas and ask for one specific experience: maybe a searchable resource guide, a client explainer, or a quiz with clickable sections.

Canvas can generate and preview HTML or React, so you can iterate on the layout and interactions. But the preview is still a draft. Test every button, check mobile behavior, confirm every factual line and link, and remove anything the model added without support.

That turns one research pass into a reusable content asset while keeping the final publish decision human.
