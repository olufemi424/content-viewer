## Final record-ready script
Instagram just made privacy edits part of Stories.

The new Pixel effect lets you describe the person or object you want pixelated before you post. Instagram promoted it for soft-launching a relationship, but the more useful creator workflow is privacy review.

Before publishing a Story, scan the entire frame for a child's face, a stranger in the background, a client name, a license plate, or anything else that should stay private. Use Pixel to obscure the specific area, then zoom in and inspect the result yourself.

That last check matters because an AI edit is not consent, and a weak blur may still leave someone recognizable from clothing, location, or context. If the subject has not agreed to appear, cropping the image or choosing another shot may be safer.

Use the effect as an editing shortcut, not a permission slip.

Save this as your pre-post privacy check.
