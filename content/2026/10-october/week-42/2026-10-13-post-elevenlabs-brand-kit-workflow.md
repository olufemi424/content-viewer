## Final record-ready script
Stop pasting your brand rules into every AI prompt.

ElevenLabs just launched Brand Kit inside ElevenCreative. You can store your colors, fonts, logos, reference images, and usage rules as one reusable object in your workspace.

Here is why that matters: repeated prompting creates drift. One teammate says “minimal,” another says “clean,” and suddenly the campaign does not look like one brand.

With Brand Kit, you type the at symbol to tag the right kit in an Image and Video prompt, a Flow, or the ElevenCreative agent. Those assets and rules then guide the generation. An agency can also keep separate kits for different clients.

A practical test is to generate one launch campaign across several formats, then check logo placement, typography, colors, and visual style against the source guidelines. This improves the starting point, but a person should still approve every final asset.

Save this before your next AI campaign build.
