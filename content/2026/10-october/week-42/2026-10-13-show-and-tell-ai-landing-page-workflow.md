## Audience + difficulty
Audience: creators, freelancers, founders, agencies, and small teams that need a credible page for an offer, launch, lead magnet, or client concept.

Difficulty: Beginner-to-intermediate. A viewer can start with a manual prompt and a static export; a developer can add Claude Code, Cursor, or a Git-based deploy after the first draft.

Final result: a live, editable landing-page preview with a clear headline, offer, proof block, CTA, responsive layout, and a branded finishing pass. The exact input is one paragraph describing the offer, audience, proof, CTA, and visual direction. The action is to turn that brief into a page draft with an AI website builder or coding agent. The output is a preview URL or local page plus editable source files. The first-three-second visual is a fast scroll across the finished hero, offer cards, proof section, and CTA, with `one-paragraph brief → live landing page` on screen.

## Final record-ready script
This is the finished result: a live landing page with a clear offer, proof section, responsive layout, and one focused call to action, built from a single paragraph. Here is the workflow. Step one, write a brief with the audience, problem, promise, proof, CTA, and visual direction. Step two, paste it into Claude Code, Cursor, Lovable, or another AI website builder and ask for an editable page, not an image mockup. Step three, inspect the first preview on desktop and mobile, then fix the hierarchy: one headline, one primary action, and no invented testimonials or numbers. Step four, replace generic copy with your approved offer, real proof, and actual links. Step five, add the finishing detail: a small branded accent, consistent spacing, and a visible focus state for the CTA. The output is a preview URL and editable source you can review before publishing. Use it for a freelance service, product waitlist, agency offer, event, or lead magnet. The AI accelerates the first draft; you still own the claims, design judgment, and final publish decision. Follow for more creative AI workflows.

## Risk check (claims needing cautious phrasing)
- Say “creates a draft” or “produces a preview,” not “guarantees conversions” or “builds a high-converting page.” The YouTube metrics show attention to tutorials, not business results.
- Do not imply every named tool has identical capabilities, pricing, export behavior, or deployment integrations; verify the chosen tool before recording.
- Use fictional or redacted client details for the demo. Do not paste confidential briefs, credentials, customer data, or unpublished claims into a public model.
- Show only approved testimonials, numbers, logos, links, and product claims. AI-generated proof is not proof.
- A preview URL is not necessarily production hosting. Label local, staging, and published states accurately.
- Reddit discussion is corroborating qualitative evidence with visible votes and comments, not a representative market survey.
