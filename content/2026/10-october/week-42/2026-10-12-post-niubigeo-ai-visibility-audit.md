## Final record-ready script
Does ChatGPT recommend you or your competitor?

NiubiGEO is an open-source tool that tests how different AI models describe your product, which competitors they mention, and what sources appear in their answers.

This matters because one polished visibility score can hide the evidence. NiubiGEO keeps the original answer, model, citations, and run history together, so you can inspect what actually happened.

Version 0.3 adds keyword monitoring. Choose a discovery, alternative, comparison, brand, or use-case query; run it hourly, daily, or weekly; then compare the latest result with the previous snapshot. The diff shows recommendations added or removed, position changes, and source-link changes.

Start with five buyer questions and two models. Review every answer before changing your content. A single response is one observation, not proof of market position, and the project labels monitoring as beta.

Save this and run a monthly AI visibility audit.

## Risk check (claims needing cautious phrasing)
- NiubiGEO's capabilities are documented by its own repository and release notes; this is not an independent performance review.
- Keyword Monitoring is labeled NEW / BETA, so its APIs and metrics may change.
- A single model answer is one observation, not a universal ranking or proof of market position.
- Diff View compares stored runs; it does not calculate a universal GEO score or prove that a content change caused a visibility change.
- Self-hosting keeps project data in an environment the operator chooses, but prompts still go to selected external model providers and can incur model or search costs.
