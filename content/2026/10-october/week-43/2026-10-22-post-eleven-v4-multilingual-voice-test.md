## Final record-ready script
Never choose an AI voice from one polished demo.

ElevenLabs recently compared Eleven v4 with competing models in blind listening tests across eight languages and reported that listeners preferred v4 for expressiveness in each test. That is a vendor result, not a universal verdict. But the testing method is useful for creators.

Take one approved thirty-second script and generate it with two voice models. Remove the model names. Then ask native listeners to score pronunciation, pacing, emotion, and voice consistency.

Why those four? Eleven v4 supports more than ninety languages, uses natural-language direction and inline audio tags, and is designed to preserve a speaker's identity across longer or multilingual work. TechCrunch also confirmed the expanded language support and expression controls at launch.

Do not change your whole voice stack because of a leaderboard. Run a blind test on the language, voice, and content you actually publish. Keep consent and final approval with a human.

Save this four-part voice test.
