## Final record-ready script
The best AI workflow keeps the receipts.

OpenAI just launched ChatGPT for Financial Services for eligible institutions. It combines selected premium datasets from providers including Daloopa, PitchBook, LSEG News, and Crunchbase with citations that link figures and claims back to specific tables and passages.

Why should a creator or solo operator care? Because the useful pattern is bigger than banking: retrieval, output, and review stay in one traceable workflow.

For example, a team can research a company, inspect the evidence behind an adjusted earnings figure, and turn the analysis into an approved Excel, Word, or PowerPoint template. A human still decides whether the source supports the conclusion before anything reaches a client.

You probably do not need the finance product. But you can copy the design: require every research claim to carry its source, generate into a fixed template, and add human approval before publishing or sending.

Save this as your audit-trail checklist.
