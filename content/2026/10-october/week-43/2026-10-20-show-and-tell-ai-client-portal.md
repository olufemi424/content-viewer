## Audience + difficulty
Freelancers, agencies, consultants, founders, and small delivery teams; beginner-to-intermediate. The viewer needs a sanitized project brief, a small project-data table, brand assets, and an AI app builder such as Softr, Emergent, or a comparable tool.

## Why now (1 sentence)
AI app builders are making it practical to turn structured project data into a client-facing portal preview, but the useful workflow is the reviewable handoff—not an AI-generated app left connected to private operations data.

## 3 hook options (<12 words each)
1. Turn one project brief into a client portal in minutes.

## Final record-ready script
This is the finished result: one approved project brief turned into a clean client portal with a project status, milestone timeline, files, next action, and a visible last-updated label. Step one, start with a small sanitized input: the project name, approved scope, milestones, file links, owner, and next client decision. Step two, ask Softr, Emergent, or your preferred AI app builder to create the portal shell from that schema. Step three, connect only the client-safe table and add the brand colors, logo, and navigation. Step four, preview the home page, milestone view, files area, and request form, then test the portal as a client rather than as an admin. Step five, run the finishing detail: a client-safe view contract that hides internal notes, margin, private comments, and unfinished work while showing the status and last update clearly. Step six, ask the project owner to approve every field, link, permission, and message before sharing the preview URL. Use this for website projects, retainers, consulting engagements, and agency handoffs. DM WORKFLOW for a free workflow audit.
