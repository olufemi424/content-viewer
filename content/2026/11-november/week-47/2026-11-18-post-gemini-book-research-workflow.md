## Final record-ready script
That business book you bought can now become cited script research. In Google Play Books, check for the Tools badge and “Use with Gemini Notebook.” Add the eligible book using the same Google account, then ask one narrow question, like: “What three mistakes does this author warn new freelancers about?” Gemini Notebook returns grounded notes with inline citations. Open every citation and compare it with the original passage before writing. If the book is greyed out, stop—it is not eligible. If you own an eligible Play Book, comment BOOK for the verification checklist.

## Risk check (claims needing cautious phrasing)
- Say `select purchased English-language ebooks`, not every Play Book; eligibility changes and some titles are unavailable because of publisher, language, or formatting restrictions.
- A `Tools` badge only indicates that a book may be eligible; confirm that `Use with Gemini Notebook` appears.
- Say responses are grounded with inline citations according to Google; do not imply that citations make every generated interpretation correct.
- Preserve the human gate: open each citation and compare it with the original passage before using the note in a script.
- Uploaded personal books in a Play Books library are not available through this Play Books source flow, according to Google Help.
- Availability may vary by account and region; this demonstration should use a verified eligible title and no sensitive notes.
