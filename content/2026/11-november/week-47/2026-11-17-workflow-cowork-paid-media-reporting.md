## Audience + difficulty
Audience: Freelance paid-media buyer or small agency account lead
Difficulty: Intermediate

## Why now (1 sentence)
Two high-view Cowork marketing tutorials and a public paid-media workflow report the same pattern: persistent context plus reusable skills can turn recurring ad analysis into a repeatable reporting job, not a fresh chat every Friday.

## 3 hook options (<12 words each)
1. Stop rebuilding the same paid-media report every week
2. Turn ad exports into a client-ready report draft
3. Your reporting skill should remember the KPI rules

## Final record-ready script
Stop rebuilding the same paid-media report every week. Create a client folder with three parts: brand context, raw Meta and Google exports, and an output folder. In Claude Cowork, add the account's KPIs, target CPA, naming rules, and last approved report. Then make one reporting skill: read the new CSVs, compare them with the previous period, flag unusual spend or conversion changes, and draft a report with a short summary, KPI table, and three recommended actions. Run it on a copy of this week's export and check the numbers against the ad platforms before anything reaches the client. Keep recommendations and budget changes human-approved. Comment REPORT for the folder structure and prompt.

## Risk check (claims needing cautious phrasing)
- The workflow is a proposed, reproducible implementation based on public tutorials and a public practitioner comment; it is not a claim of a verified customer result.
- Dara Denney's video demonstrates Claude Cowork analyzing ad libraries, generating social reports, and scheduling recurring work. It does not prove the exact Meta/Google CSV-to-client-report workflow in this brief.
- Grace Leung's video demonstrates a marketing workspace, reusable projects, live data, skills, artifacts, and scheduled tasks. It does not prove the exact report quality or time savings for every account.
- A Reddit commenter reports using skills for weekly Meta/Google reporting and client-ready decks in 30 minutes instead of 3 hours. Agent-Reach retrieved public-web page text with `completeness_verified: false`; treat this as an anecdotal, non-exhaustive signal, not a measured benchmark.
- Do not promise accurate attribution, lower CPA, time saved, or client performance improvements. Platform exports, date ranges, attribution windows, naming conventions, and missing data can change the result.
- Keep account credentials and API keys outside the working folder. Use a least-privilege, approved data path and remove personally identifying or confidential fields before testing when possible.
- The model may misread currencies, time zones, attribution windows, or column names. Verify every KPI against the source platform before sending; humans approve recommendations, budget changes, and client-facing delivery.
