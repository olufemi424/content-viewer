## Audience + difficulty
Audience: Small-business paid-search manager
Difficulty: Intermediate

## Why now (1 sentence)
Google's August 20 AI Max update added more experiment controls, while current Google Ads documentation confirms teams can test AI Max inside an existing Search campaign before deciding whether to apply it.

## 3 hook options (<12 words each)
1. Don’t switch your whole Search campaign to AI Max
2. Test Google’s AI without dropping your brand guardrails
3. Treat AI Max like an experiment, not a shortcut

## Final record-ready script
Don’t switch your whole Search campaign to AI Max. Test it first. Google Ads can split one eligible Search campaign into control and AI Max trial traffic, with brand settings available during setup. For a small-business ads manager, run one campaign, lock the conversion goal and test dates, then compare results in the experiment report. Don’t auto-apply the winner. Review search terms, spend, conversions, and brand fit before a human approves rollout. Comment TEST and I’ll send the pre-launch checklist.
