## Audience + difficulty

- **Audience:** creators, freelance video editors, DTC founders, agencies, and small marketing teams that need more product-ad variations without handing publication to a generator.
- **Difficulty:** intermediate; the viewer needs an approved product image, verified product facts, an image/video generation tool, and a review or editing step.
- **Exact input:** one approved product photo or product page, verified claims, target audience, aspect ratio, and a short offer or CTA.
- **Tool/action:** create a product reference grid, write a short approved script, generate a vertical UGC master, create platform variants, and run a human QC pass.
- **Output:** one review-ready vertical UGC ad pack: product reference, script, 9:16 master, captions or end card, selected variants, and an approval checklist.
- **Trust detail:** synthetic actors need clear labeling and permission boundaries; product appearance, claims, audio, rights, and final publication stay with a person.
- **Workflow score:** Accuracy 4/5 | Demand Evidence 5/5 | Final-Result Clarity 5/5 | Showability 5/5 | Reproducibility 5/5 | Save/Share Value 5/5 | Business Relevance 5/5 = **34/35**.

## Risk check (claims needing cautious phrasing)

- YouTube view and like counts are observed attention signals, not proof that the workflow produces sales, lower costs, or reliable performance.
- Do not promise that a generated actor, product, label, hands, or lighting will remain consistent; inspect every shot and regenerate or reject failures.
- Use only product claims supplied or approved by the brand. Do not invent testimonials, ingredients, certifications, discounts, or performance results.
- Synthetic actors must not impersonate real people without permission. Label synthetic or AI-generated content where the platform, client, or local rules require it.
- Keep music, voice, image, product, and likeness rights documented. A generated asset is not automatically cleared for commercial use.
- The workflow creates drafts and variants; a person must review the product, spoken claims, captions, rights, disclosure, and publication destination.
