## Audience + difficulty
- **Audience:** beginners deciding whether to use a personal ChatGPT workspace or a work-managed ChatGPT Business workspace.
- **Difficulty:** beginner. No code is required; the viewer needs only a ChatGPT account and one real work task to compare.
- **Input:** one low-risk example task, such as turning a messy project brief into a checklist.
- **Output:** a simple decision screen: personal ChatGPT for individual work, or a managed Business workspace for team-owned work.
- **Tools:** ChatGPT personal workspace, ChatGPT Business workspace, a sample non-sensitive brief, and the official OpenAI workspace/privacy pages.
- **Finishing detail:** show the workspace switcher and label the chat before sharing it. Never paste confidential client, company, financial, health, or credential data just to test a plan.

## Why now (1 sentence)
OpenAI’s current Business documentation describes Business as a shared organization workspace whose seats include ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex, while personal ChatGPT has separate data controls and workspace choices.[1][2]

## 3 hook options (<12 words each)
1. ChatGPT and ChatGPT Work are not the same decision.
2. Before pasting work data into ChatGPT, check the workspace.
3. The beginner’s guide to personal versus work ChatGPT.

## Final record-ready script
Are you using ChatGPT for yourself, or for work? The difference is not just a different button. Let me show you the beginner version.

Start with the same harmless task in both places: turn a short project brief into a checklist. Your personal ChatGPT workspace is for your own use, with personal data controls you manage. A ChatGPT Business workspace is a shared, organization-managed workspace. OpenAI says Business seats include ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex, so “Work” should not be confused with a separate API account.

Here is the practical rule: use personal ChatGPT for personal experiments and individual work. Use the company workspace when the work belongs to a team, needs shared context, or is governed by an administrator. Business workspace data is not used by OpenAI to train its models, according to OpenAI’s Business overview. But your organization can apply its own access, retention, and management policies.

The finishing detail is the one beginners skip: before merging a personal workspace into Business, export or save anything important. OpenAI says a merge can deactivate the personal workspace, and personal content becomes subject to the organization’s policies. Also remember: a ChatGPT Business subscription does not include API usage; the API is billed separately.

So do not ask, “Which one has the smarter answer?” Ask, “Who owns this work, who can manage it, and what data am I allowed to share?” Use a fake or redacted brief for your first test, then have a human review important outputs before they reach a customer or system. Save this before you choose a ChatGPT workspace.

## Risk check (claims needing cautious phrasing)
- Product names and seat access can change; say “OpenAI’s current Business documentation describes…” rather than presenting the labels as permanent.
- Personal ChatGPT data controls vary by account, region, product, and settings; tell viewers to check their own settings rather than promising one universal privacy behavior.[2]
- Business workspace data is not used to train OpenAI models according to the current Business overview, but an organization may still control access, retention, and managed-account data. Do not describe Business as invisible to an employer or administrator.[1][3]
- Do not say that ChatGPT Work is the same as the OpenAI API. OpenAI lists ChatGPT Work and Codex as included access within Business seats, while API usage is billed separately.[1]
- Merging a personal workspace can deactivate it and make content subject to organization policies; viewers should save important personal content first.[3]
- This post is general product education, not legal, compliance, or security advice.
