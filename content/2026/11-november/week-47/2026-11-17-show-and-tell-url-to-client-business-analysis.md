## Final record-ready script
This is the finished result: one website URL turned into a Google Doc and PDF with a business overview, target audience, positioning, customer journey, and a short list of questions to verify before a client call.

Start with a public prospect website and a simple intake form. In n8n, send the URL to Firecrawl or another permitted scraper, then pass the captured pages to an AI model such as Gemini or Perplexity. Ask for structured fields, not a vague essay: what the company sells, who it appears to serve, what makes the offer different, and which claims still need proof. Write those fields into a Google Docs template, save a PDF copy in Drive, and send yourself the link by Gmail.

The trust step matters. Open the report beside the source site, delete anything the model cannot point to, and label estimates as estimates. Keep private analytics, customer records, and login-only pages out of this pilot. Use the approved report to prepare better questions or a draft proposal, not to make a promise the source did not support. DM AUDIT for a workflow audit.
