## Final record-ready script
Stop asking AI editors for one locked export. Agent28 is now in beta, and it turns plain-English requests into a timeline you can still edit. For a freelance short-form editor, that means one raw talking-head clip can become a first pass with dead space cut, captions added, and B-roll inserted. Upload a non-sensitive test clip, request one edit at a time, then inspect every cut, caption, and asset on the timeline. Keep the version only if it matches your brief; export stays human-approved. Comment TIMELINE and I’ll send the five-point review checklist.
