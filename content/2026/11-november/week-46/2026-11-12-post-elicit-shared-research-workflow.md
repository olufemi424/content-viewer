## Audience + difficulty
Audience: Small-agency research lead
Difficulty: Intermediate; Collaborative Sessions and Shared Projects require an organization on Elicit Scale or Enterprise

## Why now (1 sentence)
Elicit launched Collaborative Sessions, reusable Skills, and Shared Projects on September 1, giving small research teams a reproducible way to keep methods, evidence, and review history together.

## 3 hook options (<12 words each)
1. Stop rebuilding your research process in every new chat
2. Give every researcher the same method and evidence trail
3. Turn scattered AI research into one reviewable team project

## Final record-ready script
Stop rebuilding your research process in every new chat. Elicit now lets teams package a repeatable method as a Skill, work with the agent in one Collaborative Session, and keep sessions, files, sources, and artifacts inside a Shared Project. For a small-agency research lead, start with one client question and one approved evidence checklist. Let two researchers explore separate threads, then compare their sources and edits in the shared history. A human still approves the final conclusion before it reaches the client. Comment RESEARCH and I’ll send the project setup checklist.
