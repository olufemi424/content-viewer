## Audience + difficulty
- **Audience:** freelancers, small agencies, and solo service businesses that spend too much time sorting job posts and rewriting the same proposal
- **Difficulty:** intermediate

## Why now (1 sentence)
Freelancers are already using AI to score opportunities and draft proposals, but the useful version is a review queue that saves the copy while keeping the final application and platform account under human control.

## 3 hook options (<12 words each)
1. Stop writing proposals for jobs you should never apply to.
2. Let AI rank the shortlist; you decide what gets submitted.
3. Turn one job post into a reviewed proposal draft in minutes.

## Final record-ready script
Stop writing proposals for jobs you should never apply to.

The expensive part of freelance prospecting is not typing. It is spending twenty minutes on a job that was a bad fit from the start, then doing it again tomorrow.

Here is the workflow I would build. Start with a job description you have copied into a form, a spreadsheet, or a manual intake box. Do not give the workflow permission to log into your account or submit anything.

First, save the job title, description, link, budget, client details if they are visible, and the date you found it. Next, ask AI to score the opportunity against your own rules: skill fit, budget, project clarity, client signals, and red flags. Make it return the score, the evidence it used, and the unanswered questions. If a fact is missing, it should say unknown instead of filling the gap.

Only pass the jobs above your threshold to the next step. Give the proposal writer three things: the job post, your approved service profile, and a small library of real proof points. The model should fill the specific parts of your template, not invent a new personality every time. It can explain the client problem, choose one relevant example, and draft a short next step.

Put the result in a sheet or review page with the original job, score, reasons, proposal, and status. Then you read it. Fix the parts that sound generic, remove claims you cannot prove, check the link, and decide whether the job is worth your connects. Only you open the platform, paste the final version, answer screening questions, and submit it.

The output is not an autopilot bidder. It is a ranked shortlist and a first draft that gets you from job post to a considered decision faster. Track how many jobs you skip, how often you edit the draft, and how many proposals lead to a reply. If the draft is not in your voice, change the examples and template before you add more automation.

## Risk check (claims needing cautious phrasing)
- Do not claim that AI-generated proposals improve hire rate, reply rate, income, or proposal quality without a measured experiment.
- Do not recommend automated login, scraping behind an account, proposal submission, messaging, or other actions that may violate Upwork rules or change as the platform changes.
- Use manually copied job text, an approved feed, or an official integration only after checking the current platform terms and permissions.
- Keep the final application, screening answers, rate, and submission with a human. The workflow should create a draft and a decision queue, not an autonomous bidder.
- The score is a recommendation, not a fact about a client. Show the evidence, mark missing fields as unknown, and let the freelancer override it.
- Use redacted job posts and synthetic profile data in the demo; do not expose client details, private messages, or account credentials.
