## Why now (1 sentence)
Meta's Muse gives US users a background agent with a dedicated cloud computer, an audit trail, and approval checks before sensitive actions such as sending email or purchasing.

## Final record-ready script
Stop babysitting vendor research in ten browser tabs. Meta says Muse keeps working after you close the app, using its own cloud computer. For a freelance event producer, that could mean researching approved venues, comparing availability and policies, then returning a shortlist with an audit trail. The useful boundary is the approval check: Muse says it pauses before sending an email or making a purchase. So start with public research, inspect every source, and approve outreach yourself. Comment MUSE and I’ll send the vendor-research checklist.
