## Final record-ready script
Here is the finished result: one 20-minute recording turned into a short clip, three post drafts, and a review-ready publishing queue. The point is not to let an agent publish whatever it wants. The point is to remove the repetitive work between recording and approval.

Start with one recording and a small brief: who it is for, the one idea you want people to remember, the channels you use, and any claims or phrases you need to avoid. Give that to Claude and ask it to propose the clip angle, the opening line, and the post variations. Keep the source recording attached so every draft can be checked against what was actually said.

Next, move the approved edit into Riverside. In the demonstrated workflow, Claude helps plan the next piece, remove false starts and disfluencies, create a vertical clip from the long recording, and prepare publishing steps. Use those stages as a checklist, not as permission to skip review. The first output to show on camera is the clip beside the transcript and the three post drafts.

Now add a simple queue. One row per asset: source timestamp, channel, draft copy, owner, status, and approval link. An n8n or Make workflow can copy approved metadata into the queue and notify the owner, but it should stop before publishing. A human checks the edit, names, numbers, quotes, music rights, and whether the post still sounds like the person who recorded it.

That is the reusable system: record once, extract a few specific moments, edit one draft, adapt it carefully, then approve each asset before it leaves the queue. DM CONTENT for the checklist and the review-column template.

## Risk check (claims needing cautious phrasing)
- Describe this as a reproducible workflow pattern, not a guaranteed way to create a week's worth of publishable content.
- The first YouTube video is sponsored and demonstrates a Claude-to-Riverside workflow; sponsorship and the creator's results should be disclosed if mentioned.
- AI may invent quotes, alter meaning, miss names or numbers, or create captions that do not match the source. Require transcript and source checks.
- Riverside, Claude, n8n, and Make features, pricing, integrations, and limits can change. Demonstrate the configured plan and label optional steps.
- Do not claim the Reddit poster's 30–40 hours per month is independently verified. Agent-Reach returned public search-results scope with incomplete comment coverage.
