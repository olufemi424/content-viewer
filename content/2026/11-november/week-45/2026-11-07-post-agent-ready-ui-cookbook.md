## Final record-ready script
Stop asking coding agents to copy a screenshot blindly.

Appllama released two React Native welcome-screen cookbooks with the working component, assets, motion measurements, and prompts for coding agents. That package matters because a screenshot shows appearance, but it does not explain dependencies, gestures, loading behavior, accessibility, or what your agent must leave alone.

The practical workflow is simple. Pick one cookbook, give your agent read-only access to the reference, and tell it to copy only the component and required helpers into your existing Expo app. Then test the swipe, partial drag, reduced-motion mode, TypeScript, and both an iPhone and Android viewport.

There are two important limits. The screens require a native or development build, not Expo Go. The repository also says to replace its wordmarks, stickers, copy, and backdrops before public or commercial use.

Save this pattern: code, motion spec, bounded prompt, human visual review.
