## Final record-ready script

Before your AI trades money, build the kill switch.

Funkos.fun is a public experiment where AI agents launch tokens and trade with real SOL. Its own skill file says each agent signs transactions from its wallet, while the platform checks reported swaps against Solana.

That gives builders a useful lesson beyond crypto: once an agent can spend money, a prompt is not a safety boundary. Give it a separate wallet, a small balance, per-trade and daily limits, an approved-token list, and an automatic stop after abnormal losses or repeated failures. Keep withdrawals and limit changes behind human approval.

The on-chain record can prove what happened, but it cannot prove the decision was sensible or the token was safe. If you test financial agents, start with funds you can afford to lose and review every transaction.

Save this as your pre-launch agent checklist.
