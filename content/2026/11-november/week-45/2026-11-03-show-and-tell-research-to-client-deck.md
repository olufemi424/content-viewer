## Final record-ready script
This is the finished result: a six-slide client update built from one source packet, with citations, a clear recommendation, and a final slide that says what happens next.

Start with a narrow input: a redacted research folder, approved company notes, and a one-paragraph brief that names the audience and decision the deck needs to support. Ask NotebookLM or your research tool to extract the facts, disagreements, and source links first. Do not ask for pretty slides yet.

Next, turn that checked brief into a slide plan: context, evidence, recommendation, trade-offs, and next action. Put one source link beside every factual claim. Then move the plan into Gamma or another presentation editor and generate the first draft. Keep the layout simple, use your own logo and colors, and leave uncertain claims marked for review.

The finishing detail is an evidence pass. Open the source beside each slide, fix a number or citation that does not hold up, and remove anything the model invented. Export the approved deck as a PDF, keep the editable source, and send it only after a human signs off.

That gives you a repeatable research-to-deck workflow for client updates, proposals, and workshops. DM WORKFLOW for a free workflow audit.
