## Final record-ready script
Stop automating tasks before you map where time disappears.

Codos calls itself a virtual Chief AI Officer. Its approach starts with AI agents interviewing employees, then maps the company context and deploys automations across different functions.

The useful lesson for a small team is the order of operations. Pick one repeated process, like client onboarding. Interview the person doing it. Capture every tool, handoff, delay, exception, and approval. Then rank the friction by hours lost and business impact.

Only automate the highest-value step. For example, an agent might collect approved client details and prepare the project brief, while a human checks the scope before anything reaches the client.

Codos says its system can run on customer-controlled infrastructure, but its impact figures are company-reported. Copy the workflow pattern, not the promises: diagnose, map, automate, measure, and keep a named owner responsible.

Save this before your next automation project.
