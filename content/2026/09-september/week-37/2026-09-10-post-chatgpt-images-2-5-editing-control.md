## Hook
AI image editing finally feels less like prompt roulette.

## Why this matters
OpenAI launched ChatGPT Images 2.5 with Sketch and comment-based edits. Creators can now show the model a rough composition, then point to one region and describe the change instead of rewriting a full prompt and hoping the rest survives.

## Mechanism
Start in Sketch to block the layout. Generate the first image, place comments directly on details that need work, and iterate. OpenAI says the model follows edits more reliably across turns and cuts generation latency by up to 50% versus Images 2.0.

## Proof/use case
Build a thumbnail by sketching the subject on one side and headline space on the other. Generate, then comment only on the face, background, or text area that missed. Axios tested the new editor on a logo, a pet-photo transformation, and a tattoo concept, corroborating the more directed workflow.
