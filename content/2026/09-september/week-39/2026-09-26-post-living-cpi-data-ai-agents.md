## Hook
This may be the most useful AI-agent job nobody is talking about.

## Why this matters
Important public data is often scattered across statistical-office sites, PDFs, and incompatible tables. That makes current, detailed inflation analysis expensive to maintain—and hard for smaller teams to build on.

## Mechanism
Harvard Business School's IEM Lab launched Living CPI Data: a global database maintained by scheduled AI agents. They find official CPI releases, extend historical series, build connectors, standardize structure while preserving originals, and send work through a visible validation queue.

## Proof/use case
At capture on September 26, the public site listed more than 10.4 million observations, over 120,000 series, and 191 economies. A data journalist or operator can move from headline inflation into official product classifications, basket weights, and even average item prices where available—without starting every investigation from a PDF hunt.

Save this if public data powers your work."
