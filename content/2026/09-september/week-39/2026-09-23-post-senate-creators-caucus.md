## Hook
Creators just got a direct policy channel into the U.S. Senate.

## Why this matters
Rules about AI replicas, platforms, and online business can reshape a creator's income and identity. The new bipartisan caucus gives creators a forum to explain those consequences before policy is written.

## Mechanism
Senators Cory Booker and John Kennedy launched the Senate Creators Caucus to hear directly from creators, educate lawmakers, and advocate for policies that recognize creators as businesses. It complements the existing House caucus, creating creator-focused forums in both chambers of Congress.

## Proof/use case
At the launch, creators discussed their business models and policy challenges. YouTube says creators also met congressional offices to support the NO FAKES Act, a proposal addressing unauthorized AI-generated replicas of a person's voice or likeness.

## Final record-ready script
"Creators just got a direct policy channel into the U.S. Senate.

This matters because rules about AI replicas, platforms, and online business can reshape your income—and even your identity.

Senators Cory Booker and John Kennedy launched the bipartisan Senate Creators Caucus. Its job is to hear directly from creators, educate lawmakers, and push for policies that recognize creators as real businesses. It complements the existing House caucus, so creator-focused forums now exist in both chambers.

And there is already a concrete use case. At the launch, creators explained how their businesses work. YouTube says creators also met congressional offices to support the NO FAKES Act—a proposal aimed at unauthorized AI-generated copies of someone's voice or likeness.

Important caveat: a caucus is a forum, not a new law. Its value depends on whether creator input changes actual legislation.

Should creators organize around AI policy—yes or no?"

## Risk check (claims needing cautious phrasing)
- Say the Senate caucus complements the existing House caucus; do not describe the caucus itself as legislation.
- Describe the NO FAKES Act as a proposal unless its legislative status changes.
- Say the proposal is aimed at unauthorized AI-generated replicas; do not imply it bans all synthetic media.
- The caucus creates a forum for access, education, and advocacy; it does not guarantee specific policy outcomes.
- Avoid treating YouTube's policy priorities as the complete agenda of the Senate caucus.
